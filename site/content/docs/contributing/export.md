_"Information networks are more resilient when they're more distributed."_

In an era where powerful people are trying to erase our history, it's more
important than ever that we keep it safe and keep telling our story. Archival
work doesn't just happen in the neoclassical marble vaults of academia; it's
happening in apartments, closets, and bedrooms around the world. In a landscape
where schools, libraries, and academic institution are under attack, guerilla
archiving is how we fight back.

You can download a full copy of the Ace Archive collection using the link below.
You can use this copy to keep a backup, share with friends, browse the
collection offline, do research, or even host your own digital archive project.
