If you want to propose content to be added to the archive, you can email us:

[contact@acearchive.lgbt](mailto:contact@acearchive.lgbt)

In your email, provide the following information. Also check out the [best
practices]({{<ref "docs/contributing/best-practices" >}}) page for more
information about what makes a good submission.

## Context and significance {#context}

What is the content you're suggesting be added to the archive? Why is it
significant to the queer community? Why should it be included in the archive?

## Sources {#sources}

Where can we find this content? Please provide URLs. In the case of published
works, we prefer more authoritative sources like journal databases, libraries,
and archives like the Internet Archive, but this can also be as simple as a link
to a forum thread or a blog post.

If the content can't be found on the web (original research, scans of physical
materials, transcripts, etc.), feel free to attach files to the email.

If the content isn't accessible or is written in a language other than English,
consider whether a transcript or a translation are available. If the content
isn't available on the web anymore, [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/)
links are fine.

## Content warnings {#content-warnings}

Are there any content warnings which should be attached to this content?

## Anything else {#anything-else}

Any additional context or information you're able to provide is helpful!
